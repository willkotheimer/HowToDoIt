## 301 — Fix a Flat  (category: Wheels & Tires)
Intro: "Repair a flat and get the wheel rolling true again."

1. **Remove the wheel** — Release the brake, open the quick-release, and lift the wheel out of the dropouts. → `301-1`
2. **Unseat the tire & pull the tube** — Work a tire lever under the bead, run it around the rim, and pull out the punctured tube. → `301-2`
3. **Patch or replace the tube** — Find the hole, buff it, and press on a patch — or swap in a fresh tube. → `301-3`
4. **Reseat the tire** — Tuck the tube in and work the bead back over the rim by hand, checking nothing is pinched. → `301-4`
5. **Inflate & remount** — Inflate to the pressure on the sidewall, drop the wheel back in, and close the quick-release. → `301-5`

## 302 — Adjust Rim Brakes  (category: Brakes)
Intro: "Dial in rim brakes for crisp, even stopping power."

1. **Check pad wear** — Inspect the pads for wear and embedded grit; replace any worn past the line. → `302-1`
2. **Align the pads** — Set each pad to strike the rim squarely, just below the tire, with a slight toe-in. → `302-2`
3. **Set the pad gap** — Balance the spring tension so both pads sit an equal, close distance from the rim. → `302-3`
4. **Tension the cable** — Pull the cable snug at the caliper and lock the pinch bolt. → `302-4`
5. **Test & fine-tune** — Squeeze the lever, spin the wheel to check for rub, and fine-tune with the barrel adjuster. → `302-5`

## 303 — Clean & Lube the Chain  (category: Drivetrain)
Intro: "Clean and lubricate the chain for a quiet, smooth-shifting drivetrain."

1. **Shift to the small cog** — Shift onto the smallest rear cog to slacken the chain for cleaning. → `303-1`
2. **Degrease the chain** — Scrub the chain with a brush and degreaser, working each link and the cassette. → `303-2`
3. **Wipe it dry** — Backpedal the chain through a clean rag until it runs dry and bright. → `303-3`
4. **Apply lube** — Drip a drop of lube onto each roller while backpedaling slowly. → `303-4`
5. **Wipe the excess** — Backpedal, then wipe off the surplus so the chain is slick but not greasy. → `303-5`
